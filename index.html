About

I'm a Preservation and Sustainability Analyst at The Community Builders, Inc. in Boston, working in Yardi, Microsoft Dynamics and Power BI on the utilities reporting behind keeping existing affordable housing livable and financially sound.

Alongside that I'm pursuing an M.S. in Data Analytics Engineering at Northeastern University, where I'm carrying a 4.0 and am part of the Data Club @ Northeastern.

Before Boston I spent ten months as a Data Analysis Engineer at The City Tutors in New York, working on the instrumentation and prediction side of a tutoring platform — distributed tracing with OpenTelemetry and Honeycomb.io across a Django application, and a model that flagged students at risk of disengaging so program coordinators could reach them before they dropped off.

What I keep coming back to is the point where messy operational data turns into a decision somebody can act on: anomaly detection, remaining-useful-life estimation, and the unglamorous cleaning work that makes either of them possible. Most recently that took the shape of DigiSight, a predictive maintenance platform for hydrogen electrolyzers that placed third at the ACWA Power Industrial Hackathon.

DIGISIGHT

ACWA Power Industrial Hackathon — Third Place, Sep 2025 – Jan 2026

An AI-powered predictive maintenance system for hydrogen electrolyzer monitoring, built as an end-to-end machine learning pipeline. Autoencoder-based anomaly detection surfaces drift before it becomes damage, and LSTM networks estimate remaining useful life across three distinct equipment failure modes. Placed third overall.

MOVIE NIGHT

Next.js 16 / React 19 · Supabase · Private

A weekly movie-picking app for a small friend group, running on a state machine driven by a daily clock tick: the week opens Monday, the night is scheduled Tuesday from a When2Meet-style availability grid, picks close Thursday, and the veto window shuts Friday. A rotation queue decides whose turn it is; attendees can veto once, forcing a single veto-proof re-pick. Built on Supabase with Postgres, row-level security and Discord OAuth, TMDB for search and posters, and a dev simulator that seeds five fake users to advance phases. Casino-table aesthetic in CSS Modules.

URINALYSIS

TensorFlow · Jupyter Notebook · Private

A TensorFlow neural network that classifies urinary tract infections from urinalysis test results. Categorical features are one-hot encoded, then passed through two dense ReLU layers of 32 and 64 units into a single sigmoid output. Trained over 800 epochs with a 20% validation split, reaching 98.08% training and 93.87% validation accuracy; comparing the adam and sgd optimizers alongside learning-rate scheduling cut validation loss by a further 10%.

SKID-DOODLE

TypeScript · Private · On hiatus

A mobile app for tracking bowel health, taking the habit-logging approach that Sleep Cycle applies to sleep. Each session is timed and then rated on shape, stench and satisfaction, with optional tags for symptoms worth watching. A social layer lets a small circle of friends react and comment on one another’s entries, each posted under a curated descriptive tag.
